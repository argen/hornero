# Launch Brief — Threadly v2

**Owner:** Marketing / Product
**Status:** Draft for exec review

## The product

Threadly v2 is a productivity platform that helps teams stay aligned. We've
rebuilt the whole thing on a modern stack and the UI is significantly faster.

## Who it's for

Anyone who needs to manage tasks, communicate with their team, and stay
productive. We see strong fit across SMB, mid-market, and enterprise — really
any team that wants to ship faster.

## What we're announcing

- **New UI** — completely redesigned, faster than before.
- **AI assistant** — drafts updates, summarises threads, suggests next steps.
- **Mobile parity** — iOS and Android apps reach feature parity with web.

## Headline messaging

> Threadly v2: The all-in-one productivity platform for modern teams.

Sub-headline:

> Faster, smarter, and built for how teams actually work.

## Channels

- Product Hunt launch day-of.
- Email blast to all existing users.
- LinkedIn organic + paid for awareness.
- Twitter/X organic.
- Press outreach (TC, The Verge, Fast Company).
