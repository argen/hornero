# PRD — Onboarding Quick-Tour

**Owner:** PM team
**Status:** Draft

## What we're shipping

A 3-step in-product tour that fires on first login and walks new users through
the dashboard, the create-project button, and the help menu. The tour can be
skipped but defaults to on.

## Why now

Activation numbers feel low. Several enterprise customers asked for "more
onboarding." Sales has been pushing for this for two quarters.

## Success criteria

We'll consider the launch successful if:

- More users complete the tour than skip it.
- Tour-completion rate is above 60% in the first month.
- The number of users who click the create-project button in their first
  session goes up.
- Sales reports better onboarding feedback in QBRs.

## Rollout

Ship to 100% of new users on day one. We can always feature-flag it off later
if there's a problem.
