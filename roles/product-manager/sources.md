# Product manager — sources

The curated bibliography for the product-manager role. Skills cite from this list. New frameworks introduced in skills must trace to a name here (or be added with rationale).

## Anchors

### Marty Cagan — Silicon Valley Product Group
- *Inspired: How to Create Tech Products Customers Love* (2008, rev. 2017)
- *Empowered: Ordinary People, Extraordinary Products* (2020, with Chris Jones)
- *Transformed: Moving to the Product Operating Model* (2024)
- **Why he matters:** Defines the modern PM role; "product discovery" vocabulary; the four big risks (value, usability, feasibility, business viability); strong stance against feature factories.
- Used in: product-discovery, prioritization, role-clarification skills.

### Teresa Torres — Product Talk
- *Continuous Discovery Habits* (2021)
- **Why she matters:** Opportunity Solution Tree (OST); the discovery cadence (weekly customer touchpoints); structured customer interviewing.
- Used in: discovery-interview-prep, opportunity-solution-tree, evidence-based-prioritization skills.

### Martin Eriksson — Mind the Product co-founder
- *Product Leadership* (2017, with Richard Banfield and Nate Walkingshaw)
- The PM Venn diagram (Tech / Business / UX) — the most-cited model of the PM role
- ProductTank, Mind the Product conference
- **Why he matters:** Defined what a PM does in a way the whole industry adopted; community-building voice in product leadership.
- Used in: role-clarification, pm-onboarding, stakeholder-management skills.

### Melissa Perri — Product Institute
- *Escaping the Build Trap: How Effective Product Management Creates Real Value* (2018)
- Product Operations as a discipline
- **Why she matters:** Names and diagnoses the "build trap" (outputs vs outcomes); architecture for product organizations at scale.
- Used in: outcomes-not-outputs, product-ops, organizational-design skills.

### Shreyas Doshi — ex-Stripe, Twitter, Google
- LNO framework (Leverage / Neutral / Overhead tasks)
- High-quality decisions methodology
- Type-1 vs Type-2 errors in product
- **Why he matters:** Sharpest contemporary voice on PM decision quality; counters productivity theatre.
- Used in: decision-quality, prioritization, pm-self-review skills.

### John Cutler — Amplitude / The Beautiful Mess
- North Star Framework
- Product systems thinking
- "Feature factory" diagnostics
- **Why he matters:** Translates systems thinking into PM practice; visual frameworks that travel.
- Used in: north-star-metric, systems-mapping skills.

### Jeff Patton
- *User Story Mapping: Discover the Whole Story, Build the Right Product* (2014)
- **Why he matters:** The canonical alternative to flat backlogs; the technique most-used to align engineering on the why behind a release.
- Used in: story-mapping, release-planning skills.

### Christina Wodtke
- *Radical Focus: Achieving Your Most Important Goals with OKRs* (2016, rev. 2021)
- *The Team That Managed Itself* (2019)
- **Why she matters:** OKR practice that actually works (vs. annual-theatre OKRs); team operating systems.
- Used in: okrs, team-cadence skills.

### Itamar Gilad
- *Evidence-Guided* (2024)
- GIST framework (Goals, Ideas, Steps, Tasks)
- Confidence Meter
- **Why he matters:** Anti-roadmap, anti-HiPPO; methodology for evidence-weighted decision-making.
- Used in: evidence-weighting, idea-triage skills.

### Clayton Christensen
- *The Innovator's Dilemma* (1997)
- *Competing Against Luck: The Story of Innovation and Customer Choice* (2016, with Taddy Hall, Karen Dillon, David Duncan)
- Jobs-to-be-Done theory
- **Why he matters:** JTBD origin; disruption theory still defines competitive analysis in product strategy.
- Used in: jobs-to-be-done, competitive-positioning skills.

### Geoffrey Moore
- *Crossing the Chasm: Marketing and Selling Disruptive Products to Mainstream Customers* (1991, rev. 2014)
- Positioning statement template ("For [target] who [need], [product] is a [category] that [benefit]")
- **Why he matters:** Adoption curve still drives go-to-market sequencing for tech products; positioning template is the industry default.
- Used in: positioning-statement, gtm-sequencing skills.

- **Roman Pichler** — *Strategize* (2016); GO Product Roadmap.
- **Ken Norton** — *Bring the Donuts*; ex-Google Ventures PM mentor.
- **Bob Moesta** — *Demand-Side Sales* (2020); JTBD in practice; switch interview technique.
- **Rich Mironov** — *The Art of Product Management* (2008); enterprise PM realism.
- **Chip Heath & Dan Heath** — *Decisive: How to Make Better Choices in Life and Work* (2013); the WRAP framework (Widen options, Reality-test, Attain distance, Prepare to be wrong). ## Curation notes

This list is capped at ~10 anchors plus a "see also." If a new entry is proposed, an existing entry must be demoted or removed — the cap exists to force prioritization. If the list grows past 15 anchors, the role is too broad and should be split.

The reason this cap matters: skills that cite from a 50-entry bibliography drift toward LLM-generated synthesis. A tight cap forces concrete sourcing.
