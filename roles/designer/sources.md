# Designer — sources

The curated bibliography for the designer role. Skills cite from this list. New frameworks introduced in skills must trace to a name here.

## Anchors

### Don Norman
- *The Design of Everyday Things* (1988, rev. 2013)
- *Emotional Design* (2004)
- **Why he matters:** Foundational vocabulary — affordances, signifiers, mappings, feedback, constraints. The error-recovery framing that underlies every modern UX heuristic.
- Used in: heuristic-review, error-state-audit, affordance-audit skills.

### Jakob Nielsen — Nielsen Norman Group
- 10 Usability Heuristics for User Interface Design (1994, periodically updated)
- *Designing Web Usability* (1999)
- NN/g articles on UX research
- **Why he matters:** The heuristic set is the industry baseline. NN/g articles are the working designer's reference library.
- Used in: heuristic-review, usability-audit skills.

### Bill Buxton — Microsoft Research
- *Sketching User Experiences* (2007)
- **Why he matters:** The "right design vs. design right" distinction; fidelity-as-tool; making interaction design tangible before code.
- Used in: prototype-fidelity, sketching skills.

### Alan Cooper
- *About Face: The Essentials of Interaction Design* (1995, rev. 2014)
- *The Inmates Are Running the Asylum* (1999)
- **Why he matters:** Personas as design tool; goal-directed design; the canonical interaction-design textbook.
- Used in: persona, goal-directed-design, interaction-patterns skills.

### Erika Hall — Mule Design
- *Just Enough Research* (2013)
- *Conversational Design* (2018)
- **Why she matters:** Practical research methods for teams that can't afford a research department; the design-as-conversation framing for chat/voice/AI surfaces.
- Used in: research-plan, conversational-flow skills.

### Steve Krug
- *Don't Make Me Think: A Common Sense Approach to Web Usability* (2000, rev. 2014)
- *Rocket Surgery Made Easy* (2009)
- **Why he matters:** The "obvious is good" principle; DIY usability testing methodology accessible to non-researchers.
- Used in: usability-audit, click-test skills.

### Edward Tufte
- *The Visual Display of Quantitative Information* (1983, rev. 2001)
- *Envisioning Information* (1990)
- *Beautiful Evidence* (2006)
- **Why he matters:** Data-ink ratio; small multiples; chartjunk; the canon for any UI that shows data.
- Used in: data-viz-review, dashboard-design skills.

### Brad Frost
- *Atomic Design* (2016) — atoms, molecules, organisms, templates, pages
- **Why he matters:** The mental model behind most modern design systems; bridges design and front-end implementation vocabulary.
- Used in: design-system, component-audit skills.

### Kat Holmes — ex-Microsoft Inclusive Design
- *Mismatch: How Inclusion Shapes Design* (2018)
- Microsoft Inclusive Design Toolkit
- **Why she matters:** Reframes accessibility from compliance to inclusion; the "solve for one, extend to many" principle.
- Used in: accessibility-audit, inclusive-review skills.

### Mike Monteiro — Mule Design
- *Ruined by Design* (2019)
- *Design Is a Job* (2012)
- **Why he matters:** Design ethics with teeth; the designer's professional responsibility for downstream harm. Counterweight to "I just made what I was asked."
- Used in: ethics-review, design-critique skills.

### Adam Wathan & Steve Schoger — Refactoring UI
- *Refactoring UI* (2018)
- **Why they matter:** Specific, actionable tactics for going from "engineer-built" to "designed" — type, spacing, color hierarchy. The most-cited practical reference for engineers learning design.
- Used in: visual-polish, hierarchy-audit skills.

## See also

- **Dieter Rams** — "Ten principles for good design" — the design canon's ethical compass.
- **Julie Zhuo** — *The Making of a Manager* (2019) — design leadership; ex-Facebook VP Product Design.
- **Frank Chimero** — *The Shape of Design* (2012) — the role of intention and craft.
- **Josh Clark** — Big Medium; AI UX, mobile-first canon (*Tapworthy*, 2010).
- **Microsoft Inclusive Design Toolkit** — companion to Kat Holmes' work.
- **Apple Human Interface Guidelines** — platform standard for Apple ecosystems.
- **Material Design** — Google's platform standard; pattern reference.
- **WCAG 2.2** — Web Content Accessibility Guidelines; the regulatory floor, not the ceiling.
- **Refactoring UI** — already in anchors; also a website with ongoing tactical posts.

## Curation notes

Cap is ~10 anchors plus "see also." Adding a new anchor requires demoting an existing one. The cap forces concrete sourcing in skills — a 50-name bibliography drifts toward generic synthesis.

The designer role spans a wide territory (visual, interaction, research, systems, ethics, inclusion). The anchor selection deliberately covers each pillar: Norman (cognition), Nielsen (heuristics), Buxton (process), Cooper (interaction), Hall (research), Krug (testing), Tufte (data), Frost (systems), Holmes (inclusion), Monteiro (ethics), Wathan/Schoger (visual tactics).
